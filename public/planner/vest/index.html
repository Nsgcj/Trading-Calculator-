Role playbook

Assign a scalp or intraday role when your planned stop and costs fit the account’s loss cap. Give swing trades an account with enough room for the wider stop and permission for the intended hold. Leave an account unassigned when the trade you want to take exceeds its limits.

Your execution plan uses Eastern Time. The Asian window runs 8–11 p.m. and London runs 2–5 a.m. For the U.S. cash open, observe from 9:30 to 9:45 a.m. At 11:30 a.m., pause new entries until 1:30 p.m. Check the live execution phase, market eligibility and holiday changes in the Trade Planner.

Scalp

Entry setup
At a marked range edge, look for a sweep, a reclaim back inside, and a confirming break away. Place the structural stop beyond the sweep.
Holding window
Minutes within an eligible execution phase. For the U.S. cash open, the first confirmation window is 9:45–10:30 a.m. ET.
Skip or exit
Skip when spread, fees or slippage leave too little room to the first target. Exit at the planned level or when the setup is invalidated.

Intraday

Entry setup
Wait for a hold outside a completed range or a pullback to a defended level. Require a confirming break and set the stop beyond the retest swing.
Holding window
Same trading day. For New York, morning continuation runs 10:30–11:30 a.m. ET and afternoon continuation runs 1:30–3 p.m. ET.
Skip or exit
Manage existing trades during downtime. Skip entries that conflict with news restrictions. Plan exits before the account’s cutoff.

Swing

Entry setup
Mark a higher-time-frame level and wait for a confirmed rejection or breakout retest in an eligible entry phase. Put the stop beyond the structure that supports the trade.
Holding window
Multiple sessions when the account permits the intended hold. Confirm overnight and weekend rules separately, and set a planned exit and next review.
Skip or exit
Before entry, model the loss at the stop including fees, slippage, and expected funding through exit. Skip if the loss exceeds the risk budget or the hold is restricted.

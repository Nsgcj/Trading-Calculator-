Execution checklist
  1. Account eligibility · Firm restrictions, drawdown and available risk.
  2. Market eligibility · Check that the asset is in its execution window.
  3. Fundamental environment · Check scheduled releases, news restrictions and intermarket conditions.
  4. Higher-time-frame structure · Establish daily, 4H and 1H context.
  5. Execution confirmation · Look for a liquidity sweep, CHoCH/BOS, displacement and a pullback.
  6. Risk and execution · Confirm the structural stop, size, targets and room to move.

Hedge Review

Review an open futures hedge against your chart level and account limits. Check how much size still fits before deciding whether to hold, trim, or exit.

Hold, trim, or exit

Before the cash open, mark the last session’s high and low and the swing that would invalidate the hedge. For a multi-day hold, keep the level tied to the original setup. Use one candle interval for your reviews.

Hold
The chart level still holds and the position fits the account’s modeled risk room.
Review or trim
Near the level, review the next completed candle. If the modeled move exceeds the account’s risk room, assess how much size to reduce.
Exit review
Look for a close through the level and follow-through. Account limits can require reducing or exiting sooner.

For the New York open, use 9:30–9:45 a.m. ET to observe price discovery. Recheck after your chosen candle closes and before a scheduled release or your account’s exit cutoff.
